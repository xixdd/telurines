## Getting started

This is pretty simple.

First open the brushes and click the folder icon.



![1](./images/imgbf/1.png)




Now give the folder a name, in this case I'll use "folder1" but you can use whatever you want.



![](images/imgbf/2.png)



___


Once created, select the brush you want and click the add icon (+).



![](images/imgbf/3.png)



There, make sure to turn on the toggle and select continue



![](images/imgbf/4.png)



And done, now you'll have your brush copied into the folder you made.



![](images/imgbf/5.png)



___

To delete a brush from the folder, open the menu (3 lines at the bottom), and choose the third option



![](images/imgbf/6.png)
