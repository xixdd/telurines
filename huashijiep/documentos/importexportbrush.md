## Importing and exporting brushes in 画世界Pro。

I always compare this app to ibis paint, because I believe 99% of people (who draw digitally) has used it at least once or twice. So this time I'm gonna mention it to start the tutorial.

Unlike IbisPaint, HuaShijie Pro does not use QR codes to export or import brushes. That's not necessarily a advantage or anything, just a difference. See, what it uses instead, are brush files. Every software usually has its own format of these files, that also is usually accepted only by that same software; however, there's a major exception, the Adobe Photoshop brush format ABR, which is probably the most compatible format.

And it works on Huashijie Pro too, so I'm going to use that format as an example to explain how the importing works.

### Exporting brushes (导入画笔)

For this, you will need to get your file first, which you can do by just looking up 

`"free abr brushes"`

Yeah just like that, on your favorite browser. And then look for the result that looks the best for you. 

I downloaded some from

`brusheezy.com`

But you can also get some (fewer though) from the official Photoshop site [here](https://www.adobe.com/uk/products/photoshop/photoshop-brushes.html).

___
*Note: On these sites, you'll usually get a pack of brushes, though you can also find individual ones.
___
Note: Even though ABR brushes are compatible with other software, that doesn't mean they'll work exactly as they'd in Photoshop. This because they don't have the same engine. *
___
![](./images/ieb/ieb0.webp)
Okay, so once you've got your brushes downloaded, let's open 画世界Pro.

Just open the brushes and look for the hamburger menu at the bottom right (3 lines), and you're gonna look for the second option from top to bottom.

<p class="ntms" align="center"> 导入画笔 </p>

![](./images/ieb/ieb1.webp)

A file explorer will open and you'll just have to find your file. After that, confirm and you're all done.

### Exporting (导出画笔)

This one's much simpler, though there's a very important note: <mark>You can only import brushes in the 画世界 format</mark>, which is .BRU. It works exactly the same though.

In the same menu we saw before, choose instead the 3rd option from bottom to top:

<p class="ntms" align="center">导出画笔</p>

Then it will let you give it a name and you'll see the directory to find it later. You can share it like that, but remember: because <mark>exporting is only</mark> avalibe in <mark>.bru format, it will work exclusively for HuaShijie Pro</mark>, as far as I know.
